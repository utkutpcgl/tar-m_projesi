1. Carrier-independent bay cassette

The reusable unit of architecture is one bay cassette, not one camera and not one nozzle.

Each cassette owns:

imaging enclosure support;

fixed camera-to-intervention mechanical reference;

local intervention mounting datum;

working-height adjustment;

ground-following interface;

local identity plate;

camera and controller cable routing;

local safety-state input;

compute or dedicated USB-root mounting zone;

calibration and daily-registration fiducial locations.

The carrier owns:

tractor, boom or trailer attachment;

gross lift and transport;

structural support;

operating-height coarse placement;

power source;

operator controls and E-stop routing;

travel encoder installation;

transport locks, folding and deployed-state detection.

The sensor/light lane owns the camera, lens, enclosure and illumination internals. The intervention lane owns nozzle, valve, footprint and deposition behavior. This carrier part exposes only mounting, power, timing, position and safety interfaces to those lanes.

2. Proof carrier mechanical concept

The rear three-point proof toolbar shall have:

an exact-host hitch adapter;

anti-sway restraint;

a fixed operating top-link or equivalent pitch constraint;

a transverse proof bar;

one removable bay cassette;

passive vertical compliance;

one or two gauge wheels positioned outside the action-safe region;

an operating-height hard stop;

a transport-height mechanical lock;

a sensed operating/deployed state;

an accessible E-stop;

a compute/power tray whose mass and location are included in hitch and axle calculations.

The local camera-to-intervention frame shall not flex or articulate relative to itself during operation. Ground following occurs between that local frame and the carrier adapter.

The bay shall be positioned in a lane not directly trafficked by the tractor wheels. If no rear mounting position can satisfy this on the exact host, the front three-point challenger is opened rather than accepting disturbed or occluded targets.

3. Proof travel source

The baseline travel source is a passive, ground-contact quadrature encoder mounted to a gauge or metering wheel.

It is selected because it is local, directly observable and independent of undocumented tractor CAN or display-speed behavior.

The following remain challengers only:

tractor axle or transmission encoder;

radar ground-speed sensor;

optical ground-speed sensor;

fused dual-source encoder.

A challenger replaces the passive wheel only if the wheel cannot pass the existing ≤1 mm/m scale-error, timing, stale-data and direction tests. Tractor GPS or displayed speed may be logged but may not schedule actuation.

4. Scale-up boom architecture

A qualifying existing boom retrofit shall use repeated local bay cassettes. The boom shall not be treated as the camera-to-nozzle reference.

Each boom section shall provide:

a documented local load path;

a deployed hard stop or measured repeatable deployed geometry;

a local ground-following subframe;

a fold/deploy state sensor;

a compute or USB-root location satisfying the ≤3 m cable contract;

power and E-stop distribution;

a section identity tied to calibration;

no direct structure, hose or wheel intrusion into the action-safe swath.

Any articulation between bays must preserve:

camera pitch ≤430 mm;

safe overlap ≥10 mm;

continuous ambient-light rejection;

valid deployed calibration;

no-fire while folded, moving between states or not hard-seated.

An existing boom’s chemical tank, pump, lines and nozzles are out of scope and receive no readiness credit.

TCO formulas

For candidate c, scenario s and evaluation life Y:

TCO(c,s,Y) = incremental_host_capex + carrier_capex + integration_NRE + external_pass_through_capex + Y × annual_fixed_cost + Y × annual_hours × hourly_variable_cost + Y × expected_downtime_cost - residual_value

Report these components separately. Do not hide them behind one blended rate.

scenario_coverage_ha_h = gross_coverage_ha_h × field_efficiency_sensitivity

coverage_cost_per_ha = TCO / (Y × annual_hours × scenario_coverage_ha_h)

If the denominator is zero or unknown, output null.

For two eligible candidates A and B:

break_even_hours = (fixed_cost_A - fixed_cost_B) / (hourly_cost_B - hourly_cost_A)

Only calculate when the denominator is positive and all inputs are present.

Cost treatment

Sunk host acquisition cost is excluded from incremental capex but host operating and opportunity cost remain visible.

A newly purchased host includes full acquisition, financing assumption and residual value.

Rental scenarios use dated rental quotes.

Operator cost remains in every scenario because autonomy is excluded.

Fuel or electricity uses dated scenario inputs.

Chemical, herbicide savings, yield, weed-control benefit and crop-damage benefit are excluded.

Public prices and budgetary allowances remain ranges.

Missing values remain null.

Quote expiry follows the vendor’s stated validity; when unstated, a quote older than 90 days is marked stale.

Decision method

Do not use a weighted score that permits low cost to compensate for a failed interface or safety boundary.

Apply candidates lexicographically:

Frozen-interface hard gates

Exact-host structural and operational eligibility

Evidence completeness

Five-year TCO range

Integration complexity and reversibility

Deterministic tie-break

A candidate with any hard-gate failure is rejected before cost comparison.

A TCO result is considered decisively lower only when the compared ranges support at least a 10% advantage. Within a ±10% tie band, choose the candidate with:

fewer new chassis, propulsion and road systems;

fewer safety-critical subsystems;

lower irreversible integration NRE;

easier removal and restoration of the host;

fewer calibration-changing joints.

This tie-break favors a qualifying existing-boom retrofit over a new trailer, and a rear three-point proof bar over front, trailed or self-propelled proof carriers.

Hard eligibility gates

Every candidate must satisfy or explicitly remain UNQUALIFIED on:

Frozen geometry

one-bay safe width ≥444.375 mm;

multi-bay pitch ≤430 mm;

overlap ≥10 mm;

required enclosure volume;

no carrier intrusion into the calibrated FOV;

working-height support compatible with the frozen optical range;

deployed geometry repeatable enough to pass physical calibration and registration.

Carrier kinematics

controlled operation at both 0.5 and 1.0 m/s;

valid signed travel direction;

encoder resolution ≤1 mm/count;

encoder scale error ≤1 mm/m;

encoder stale no-fire ≤5 ms;

no uncontrolled carrier articulation in the local camera-to-intervention frame.

Data and compute

dedicated USB3 root for every camera;

camera cable ≤3 m;

one proven compute lane per bay unless a replacement benchmark exists;

no unbenchmarked shared RTX 3090 assumption;

same-event hardware trigger and encoder latch.

Registration and actuation

physically measured camera-to-intervention offset;

no CAD-assumed offset;

daily registration drift threshold ≤2 mm;

deadline-abort support;

pending-command cancellation on reverse, lift or fold;

no neighboring-bay coverage reassignment.

Structural evidence

exact host and configuration identified;

exact load position included;

mass and center of gravity included;

hitch, axle, drawbar or boom rating supported by an exact primary source or signed engineering analysis;

deployed and transport conditions both considered;

missing local boom payload data is a failure, not an invitation to extrapolate.

Electrical and safety

power source and continuous rating known;

compute power measured or conservatively bounded;

fused branches;

E-stop hard cut;

default no-fire on controller or power fault;

deployed-state interlock;

no chemical-enable path.

Economic evidence

dated prices or explicit ranges;

integration labor included;

host downtime or conversion cost included;

no unsupported labor saving;

no chemical-benefit credit.

Quantitative challenger triggers
Rear to front three-point proof

Open the front three-point challenger only if an exact rear host fails one of these conditions:

no rear hitch or no documented capacity for the measured proof assembly;

no rear position keeps the safe swath outside the tractor wheel paths;

rear placement creates a repeatable optical, encoder, blur or registration failure against the existing physical thresholds;

camera cable, compute placement or power cannot satisfy the frozen interface;

rear geometry cannot maintain the operating enclosure height;

rear mounting prevents an accessible E-stop or safe transport lock.

Front mounting wins only if it passes all hard gates and its five-year proof TCO is within the 10% tie band of the rear alternative, or no rear alternative is eligible.

Existing boom to trailed scale carrier

The existing-boom retrofit remains selected only when:

an exact available boom exists;

exact local load and moment evidence exists for every proposed section;

deployed and folded configurations are eligible;

every camera has a valid local compute/USB-root route;

local ground-following preserves the optical geometry;

fold/deploy repeatability can be tied to calibration identity;

no existing hose, nozzle or structure enters the safe swath;

the retrofit TCO is no more than 10% above the trailed candidate under the same scenario.

Select the trailed fallback when:

no exact boom exists;

boom load evidence is absent;

structural modification would invalidate the host’s documented configuration;

fold joints cannot repeat the calibrated geometry;

cable or power routing requires relaxing a frozen interface;

local ground following cannot be achieved;

boom retrofit nominal TCO exceeds the trailed candidate by more than 10%;

seasonal conversion downtime makes the trailer decisively cheaper.

Rear three-point replication bridge

A two-bay rear toolbar may proceed only after:

one-bay physical A–E passes;

a second independently supported compute lane exists or a new two-camera benchmark passes;

both bays and the overlap meet the frozen geometry;

the exact host has documented capacity for full measured mass and moment;

the continuous enclosure and ground-following arrangement remain buildable;

no purchase or field claim is inferred from the survey result.

Do not extrapolate a two-bay result to three or more bays.

Travel-encoder challenger

Replace the passive wheel encoder only if it fails any of:

≤1 mm/count;

≤1 mm/m scale error;

p95 trigger alignment ≤100 µs;

maximum trigger alignment ≤250 µs;

stale-data no-fire ≤5 ms;

valid direction through stop and reversal;

repeatability across the intended controlled surface.

The replacement must pass the same gates; GPS or tractor display speed receives no exception.

Self-propelled reopening rule

Keep self-propelled closed unless all are true:

a non-autonomous, manually controllable base platform already exists as a sourced product;

tractor rear, front, boom and trailed candidates all fail a material access, compaction, geometry or host-availability requirement;

owner-supplied utilization is available;

five-year self-propelled TCO is at least 20% lower than the best eligible tractor/trailer alternative after applying +20% NRE sensitivity;

no autonomy labor saving is counted;

propulsion, steering, braking and machinery-safety work is accepted as a separate bounded part.

Reopening sensor or light selection

Carrier inconvenience is not a trigger.

The sensor/light lane may be reopened only by its own evidence contract. A carrier that cannot preserve the frozen module is rejected or redesigned.
